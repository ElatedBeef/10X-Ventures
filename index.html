What We Build

Training pilots. Advancing technology. Shaping the future of personal and regional air mobility.

✈

eVTOL Flight Academy

Professional pilot training programs focused on electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft. From private pilots to commercial operators, we prepare the next generation for the new era of aviation.

🛫

Introduction Flights

Experience the future firsthand. Our discovery and introductory flights give future pilots, investors, and enthusiasts a real taste of eVTOL technology in a controlled, professional environment.

🛰

Aerospace Development

On-site research, prototyping, and collaboration with industry partners. We are building the infrastructure and talent pipeline that advanced air mobility needs to scale.

80

Acres of Purpose-Built Campus
